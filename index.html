HydroTwin Energy

Wholesale electricity price forecasting — the energy sibling of HydroTwin, the river-gauging digital twin. River flow, snowpack, soil moisture and weather are combined with EIA energy data to forecast hub prices 1–7 days ahead.

HydroTwin Energy is a separate twin with its own deployment. It does not modify HydroTwin. Version 1 is charts, features and forecasts — 3D/Cesium scenery is out of scope. Forecasts are model estimates from a static data snapshot, not trading advice.
